# Privacy notice

the operator of RealEstate AI

## Who we are

the operator of RealEstate AI runs RealEstate AI, a service that helps people find property and contact owners and agencies. This notice explains what personal data we process and why. The service is for people aged 18 or older.

## What we collect

Account: your email address and a hashed password. Sessions: the browser name and the times you logged in. Listings you publish: text, photos, map locations, tour and video links. Conversations: the messages you send to the assistant and its replies. Enquiries: the name, phone number or email, message and preferred visit time you send to a listing, and a record of when you gave consent and which wording you saw. Usage: counters for the AI features you use. We do not store your IP address in our database. Short-lived technical counters that stop abuse may use it. Reports: if you report a listing, the reason and any note you write. We do not tell the owner who reported. Saved searches: the filters you save and how often you want email alerts. Saved listings: the listings you save with the heart button. WhatsApp: if you message our WhatsApp number, we receive your phone number and your messages, and the replies are written by an AI service. We delete the conversation 90 days after your last message. Send STOP to stop replies or DELETE MY DATA to erase the conversation. If you connect WhatsApp in the app, we send your saved-search alerts and visit reminders there until you send STOP or disconnect. Voice: if you use the microphone, your recording is sent to our speech recognition provider to be turned into text. We do not keep the recording; the text is saved in your chat history like a typed message. If you send a voice note, it is converted to text by our speech recognition provider; we keep the text, not the audio. If you ask for spoken answers, the assistant's reply text is sent to our text-to-speech provider to be read aloud; we do not keep the audio. When you send a voice note, we may reply with a voice note too: the reply text is sent to our text-to-speech provider, and we do not keep the audio. When you open a listing, we count it as one view of that listing for that day. To avoid counting the same visit twice, your IP address and browser details are used only in hashed form, with a key that changes daily, and are discarded within two days. View counts are anonymous and shown only to the listing's owner or agency.

## Why we use it

To run your account, show your listings, deliver your enquiries to the owner or agency you contacted, send the service emails you need or chose, and keep the service secure.

## Who can see it

Everyone can see the listings you publish and the agency name shown on them. When you send an enquiry, the listing's owner or the agency's team can see your name, contact details, message and, if you chose to include it, a short summary of your chat. Service providers process data for us: hosting, email delivery, AI services and map services. Listings are public web pages: search engines can index them, and apps like WhatsApp can show a preview with the title, price and first photo.

## AI features

When you use the assistant or the listing drafting tool, your messages, photos and notes are sent to an AI service provider to produce the answer or draft. AI output can be wrong, and owners must check drafts before publishing. We do not train our own models on your content.

## How long we keep it

Account data and conversations are kept until you delete them or your account. Enquiries are kept by the recipient until they delete them or you withdraw consent. When you withdraw consent or delete your account, your details are removed from the enquiries you sent. Login sessions and one-time links are removed shortly after they expire. Email delivery logs are kept for 90 days, and a minimal record that a privacy request (such as an export or deletion) took place, without your email address, is kept for up to three years.

## Your rights

You can download your data, correct it (edit it in the app or contact us), erase it by deleting your account, withdraw consent for an enquiry you sent, and nominate another person to exercise these rights for you (contact us). Look in Account, then Your data and privacy. We answer requests within the time the law allows, at most 90 days, and aim to do it much sooner.

## Contact and complaints

For any privacy request or complaint write to guptakarang2002@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change. This is version 2026-11-v13.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account and personal data at any time in the app: **Settings → Your data and privacy → Delete my account**. You can also email guptakarang2002@gmail.com and we will act on your request within the time the law allows, at most 90 days.
